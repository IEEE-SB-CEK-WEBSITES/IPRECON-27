Getting Started

Welcome, International Participants

We are pleased to welcome international students, researchers, academicians, and professionals to IPRECON 2027 in Kerala, India. To facilitate your participation, we have outlined the necessary visa information and procedures below. Please read the following instructions carefully to ensure a smooth application process.

Important Notice

Please do not apply for a tourist visa, as it is not valid for conference participation and may lead to rejection or unnecessary complications and expenses.

Visa Type: e-Conference Visa

Eligibility & Duration

International participants attending IPRECON in person must apply for an Indian e-Conference Visa — intended specifically for those visiting India for conferences, workshops, and similar academic or professional gatherings.

Who Is Eligible?

  • A foreigner whose sole purpose of visiting India is to attend a conference / seminar / workshop held in India
  • Holds a valid passport and a re-entry permit if required by your home country
  • Not persona-non-grata to the Government of India
  • Not considered an undesirable person
  • A person of assured financial standing

Duration of the Visa

Conference visas are issued for the duration of the conference and the travelling time. Delegates may also combine tourism with attending the conference.

Maximum validity: 6 months

The process is relatively straightforward — follow the instructions carefully and submit all required documentation. For questions, contact the Indian Mission in your home country.

Step by Step

Application Process

To apply for the e-Conference Visa, follow the four steps below in order.

1
Step One

Complete Registration

Only participants whose papers have been accepted and who have completed the full registration process will receive an official Conference Invitation Letter from the organizing committee. This letter is required for the visa application.

2
Step Two

Prepare Required Documents

You will need to upload the following during the application:

  • Passport-sized photograph (JPEG, white background, max 1MB, min 350×350 px)
  • Scanned copy of passport (PDF, photo/bio page only, max 300KB)
  • Conference Invitation Letter (issued by IPRECON organizing committee)
3
Step Three

Apply Online

Submit your application through the official Government of India e-Visa portal. Select the "e-Conference Visa" category and complete all required fields.

4
Step Four

Visa Approval & Travel

Processing time typically ranges from 12 hours to a few days. Upon approval, you will receive a PDF copy via email. Print this document and carry it during travel. Special e-Visa counters are available at major Indian airports.

Country-Specific Guidelines

As per the visa regulations of the Government of India, invitation-letter eligibility varies by country.

Invitation Letter Restrictions

The IPRECON organizing committee will not provide conference invitation letters to individuals holding citizenship of the following countries:

Pakistan Bangladesh Afghanistan Iraq Sudan Iran Syria Stateless persons

Eligible Countries

Conference invitation letters will be issued only to participants from countries listed as eligible under the Indian Government's e-Visa program.

All nations listed under the Indian Government e-Visa program Delegates with an accepted paper and completed registration

Nepal & Bhutan are visa-exempt and are covered separately — see Visa Exemption below.

Persons of Pakistani Origin

Individuals of Pakistani origin, irrespective of their current citizenship, are not eligible to receive visa invitation letters from the organizing committee. "Pakistani origin" refers to individuals who currently hold or previously held Pakistani citizenship, or whose parents or grandparents were citizens of Pakistan.

No Visa Needed

Visa Exemption — Nepal & Bhutan

Delegates from two neighbouring countries may attend IPRECON 2027 in person without applying for any Indian visa.

Nepal Bhutan

Freedom of Movement Agreement

Citizens of Nepal and Bhutan do not require a visa to enter India, in accordance with the Freedom of Movement Agreement between these countries and the Republic of India. Along with citizens of India, they may attend in person without the need for a visa.

No e-Conference Visa application required — proceed directly to registration.

What You Still Need

  • An accepted conference paper and completed IPRECON 2027 registration
  • Valid photo identification accepted at the India border (passport or approved national ID)
  • Your registration confirmation for entry to the conference venue
  • Accommodation booking — see the Accommodation page for delegate rates

Nepali and Bhutanese delegates are treated the same as domestic delegates for on-site registration. No invitation letter is needed for immigration purposes.

Common Questions

Visa FAQ

Quick answers to the questions the visa helpdesk receives most often.

Q1 When should I start my visa application?
Begin as soon as your paper is accepted and your registration is complete — the invitation letter is issued only after full registration. Although e-Visa processing is often 12 hours to a few days, applying at least 4–6 weeks before travel leaves room for document corrections.
Q2 Can I travel on a tourist visa instead?
No. A tourist visa is not valid for conference participation and may lead to rejection at immigration or complications on arrival. Select the e-Conference Visa category only.
Q3 Who issues my invitation letter?
The IPRECON 2027 organizing committee, after your paper is accepted and registration is paid in full. Letters cannot be issued for unregistered or unaccepted submissions, or to citizens of the restricted countries listed above.
Q4 Do Nepali and Bhutanese delegates need anything from the committee?
No visa and no invitation letter are needed for immigration. Bring valid photo identification and your registration confirmation. See the Visa Exemption section above.
Q5 My visa was refused. What happens to my paper?
Contact the committee immediately with your paper ID. Under the Remote Presentation Policy you may present online — registration fees, review outcome and IEEE Xplore submission are unaffected.
Q6 Can I extend my stay for tourism?
Yes. Conference visas are issued for the conference duration plus travel time, with a maximum validity of 6 months, and delegates may combine tourism with attendance. Confirm exact conditions with the Indian Mission in your home country.

Ready to Apply?

Visit the official Government of India e-Visa portal to begin your application. Make sure you have your Conference Invitation Letter and required documents ready.
