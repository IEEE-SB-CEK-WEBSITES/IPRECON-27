About IPRECON 2027

Connect your organization with a global community of researchers, engineers, and industry professionals.

IPRECON 2027 is a premier IEEE-sponsored international conference on power systems, renewable energy, and sustainable technologies, hosted at the College of Engineering Karunagappally, Kerala. As India's First and Only IEEE Club of 6 Technical Paper Conference, it unites ten IEEE Technical Societies under one roof.

Partnering with IPRECON 2027 connects your organization with a global community of researchers, engineers, and industry professionals — aligning your brand with innovation, academic excellence, and the trusted IEEE name.

IPRECON 2027 is part of the prestigious "Club of Six" IEEE IAS/PES international conferences in India.
