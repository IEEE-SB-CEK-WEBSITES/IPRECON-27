Requirements

Paper Requirements

6 pages max

Maximum allowable length for a paper, including references, is six pages.

3 MB limit

File size must not exceed 3 MB and must be in PDF format, without encryption or password protection.

2× blind review

A double-blind review process will be conducted for every submitted manuscript.

20% to IAS periodicals

Of presented conference papers eligible for further review and possible publication in IEEE IAS Periodicals.

Submissions with a high similarity index will not be considered for review. All accepted papers will be submitted for inclusion in the IEEE Xplore® Digital Library, subject to IEEE quality standards.

Step by Step

Paper Submission Process

Six steps through the official Microsoft CMT system.

1

Open the portal

Go to the official IPRECON 2027 submission page on Microsoft CMT. The link is published here when the portal opens.

2

Find the conference

Locate the IPRECON 2027 emblem. New users select Register at the bottom of the dialog box.

3

Create your account

Complete registration by following the on-screen instructions. Login credentials are issued on success.

4

Log in

Sign in with your registered email address and password to reach the submission dashboard.

5

Fill the submission form

Enter title, abstract and author details. Every co-author needs name, designation, affiliation and country.

6

Notify co-authors

CMT sends no automatic confirmation email. The submitting author notifies co-authors from the submission page.
