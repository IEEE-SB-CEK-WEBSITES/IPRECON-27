Theme & Scope

Next-Generation Energy Systems: Intelligence, Resilience and Decarbonization

IPRECON 2027 is the 5th edition of India's first and only IEEE Club of 6 Technical Paper Conference, organized by the IEEE Student Branch of the College of Engineering Karunagappally. With technical co-sponsorship from IEEE IAS and IEEE PES, it is a premier platform for students, researchers, and professionals to present cutting-edge work.

Before You Submit

Submission Rules

Paper Format

  • 6 pages in standard IEEE conference format
  • Extendable to 8 pages with per-page charge
  • References counted within the page limit
  • No author biography section

Conference Mode

  • Hybrid — in-person at CEK or online
  • Platform details sent after registration
  • Full access to sessions, keynotes, tutorials
  • Remote presentation for visa-restricted authors

Publication

  • IEEE Xplore® Digital Library inclusion
  • Top 20% invited for IEEE IAS periodicals
  • Subject to IEEE quality standards
  • Rigorous peer review

Registration

  • One full author registration per paper
  • IEEE membership validation required
  • Student ID verification for student rates
  • Retain your payment transaction ID
