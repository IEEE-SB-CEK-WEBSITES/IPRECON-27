Registration Guidelines

Important Information

  • The conference will be held in Hybrid Mode.
  • The maximum page limit is six pages including references. No author biography at the end.
  • Additional pages up to 8 are permitted, subject to payment of ₹1,000 ($25) extra per additional page.
  • Platform details for virtual attendees will be provided via email after registration.
  • Attendee registration includes access to all technical sessions and keynotes.

For Authors

  • Each paper must be covered by a full IEEE member or non-member registration fee by one of the authors.
  • IEEE members must provide their membership number and upload their IEEE membership card during registration if registering under the IEEE category. Validation is done after registration; failing which the balance fee of the relevant category applies.
  • Students must upload a valid student ID card if registering under the student category; failing which the balance fee applies.
  • Keep a copy of the transaction ID generated by the payment gateway and your bank — you may need it to track your payment.
  • All correspondence regarding your registration should contain your Paper ID and registration confirmation number.

For Co-Authors (Co-Presenters)

  • This registration is only for co-authors who wish to present the paper and obtain a presentation certificate.
  • It is valid only if the paper is already registered with a primary author.
  • The co-presenter can avail the presentation certificate only by actively engaging in the Q&A session after the video presentation.
  • Co-author registration payments are non-refundable.
  • Attach the payment receipt of the primary registered author to complete co-author registration.

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

  • Refunds are made after deduction of a cancellation charge (50% of the registration fee) if written notification is received before the stated cutoff. No refunds after that date.
  • Cancellation must be notified in writing by email to the organizers.
  • Bank service charges and administration fees are deducted from the refund; refunds are processed only after the conference.
  • Each accepted paper must have at least one author present. Non-presented papers will not be submitted to IEEE Xplore® as per IEEE's no-show policy.
How It Works

Registration Steps

1

Choose Registration

Click 'Register Now' and choose your category. Buy one registration per paper. Over-length manuscripts beyond six pages require ₹1,000 ($25) per additional page.

2

Enter Buyer Details

Enter buyer details (name, email, phone). Anyone can buy the registration ticket. Note that the invoice is issued in the name of the buyer.

3

Enter Attendee Details

Enter attendee details. The first attendee is the registering author. Provide the correct Paper ID, review the payment page, then proceed to pay.

For any questions regarding the registration process, fees, or payment methods, please contact us.
