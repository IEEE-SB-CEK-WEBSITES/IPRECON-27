Read Before Paying

The Rulebook

01Important Information

Applies to every registration, whichever category you choose.

  • The conference is held in hybrid mode.
  • Papers are limited to six pages including references, with no author biography at the end.
  • Up to eight pages are permitted on payment of ₹1,000 ($25) per extra page.
  • Platform details for virtual attendees are emailed after registration.
  • Attendee registration includes access to all technical sessions and keynotes.
  • Fees exclude payment gateway and currency conversion charges.
02Authors

Every accepted paper needs one author registered in full before it enters the program.

  • Each paper must be covered by a full IEEE member or non-member registration paid by one of the authors.
  • IEEE members supply their membership number and card at registration. Validation happens afterwards — if it fails, the balance fee for the relevant category becomes payable.
  • Students upload a valid student ID for the student rate, otherwise the balance fee applies.
  • Keep the transaction ID from the payment gateway and your bank in case the payment needs tracing.
  • Quote your Paper ID and registration confirmation number in all correspondence.
03Co-Authors

Co-presenter registration is only for co-authors who want to present and receive a certificate. All co-authors get full session access either way.

Conditions

  • Valid only if the paper is already registered by a primary author.
  • The certificate is issued only if the co-presenter joins the Q&A session after the presentation.
  • Co-presenter payment is non-refundable.
  • Author and co-presenter must both attend the session, physically or virtually.

Documents needed

  • Primary author's name
  • Primary author's transaction receipt
  • Paper ID
  • IEEE membership card (IEEE rate)
  • Student ID valid for 2027 (student rate)

If any condition is unmet, the committee may withhold the certificate of presentation and the co-presenter registration becomes invalid.

04Cancellation & IEEE No-Show

Written notice is required. Refunds are processed only after the conference.

  • Cancellations refund the fee less a 50% cancellation charge, if written notice arrives before 15 September 2027. No refunds after that date.
  • Notify the organizers at registrations@iprecon.org.
  • Bank service and administration charges are deducted from the refund.
  • Non-presented papers are not submitted to IEEE Xplore®, as per IEEE no-show policy.
05How to pay

Three steps on the ticketing platform.

  1. Step 01

    Choose Registration

    Pick your category. One registration covers one paper — buy additional registrations for additional papers. Over-length manuscripts need ₹1,000 per extra page ($25 for foreign authors).

  2. Step 02

    Enter Buyer Details

    Buyer name, email and phone number. Anyone can buy the ticket — note the invoice is issued in the buyer's name.

  3. Step 03

    Enter Attendee Details

    The first attendee is the registering author. Provide the correct Paper ID, click Continue, check the payment page, then Proceed to Pay.
