About the Conference

About IPRECON 2027

The IEEE International Power and Renewable Energy Conference (IPRECON 2027) is the 5th edition of India's first and only IEEE Club of 6 Technical Paper Conference, organized by the IEEE Student Branch of the College of Engineering Karunagappally (CEK), in association with the institution.

With technical co-sponsorship from the IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) and the IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES), IPRECON 2027 serves as a premier platform for students, researchers, and professionals to share cutting-edge innovations in power and renewable energy systems.

Held at the College of Engineering Karunagappally, Kerala, India, the conference features technical sessions, keynote talks, distinguished lectures, tutorials, and workshops — all centred around the theme "Advancing Power and Renewable Energy Systems through AI, Computing, and Emerging Technologies."

All accepted papers will be submitted for inclusion in the IEEE Xplore® Digital Library, subject to IEEE quality standards. The conference will be conducted in hybrid mode — welcoming participants both in-person and online.

All accepted papers submitted to the IEEE Xplore® Digital Library. Top 20% of presented papers eligible for IEEE-IAS Transactions and Magazine publication.

Hybrid Conference — Attend in-person at CEK, Kerala or participate fully online from anywhere in the world.

Reasons to Participate

Why Attend IPRECON 2027?

IEEE Xplore Publication

All accepted and presented papers are submitted to the IEEE Xplore Digital Library — the global standard for peer-reviewed engineering and technology research.

Top 20% — IAS Journal

The top 20% of presented papers receive a direct invitation for extended publication in the prestigious IEEE Industry Applications Society Transactions and Magazine.

Global Research Network

Connect with researchers, engineers, and academics from 30+ countries. Build international collaborations that span institutions and industries.

Frontier Research

Explore leading-edge work in AI-powered grids, advanced renewables, EV integration, energy storage, and power electronics from leading researchers worldwide.

Student Support Grants

Dedicated housing and travel support grants are available for eligible student presenters. IPRECON actively invests in enabling the next generation of researchers.

Kerala, God's Own Country

Attend at the College of Engineering Karunagappally in Kerala — renowned for its natural beauty, culture, and warm hospitality. An experience beyond the conference hall.
