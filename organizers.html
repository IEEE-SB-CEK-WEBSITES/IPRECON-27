Host Institution

College of Engineering Karunagappally

Government College of Engineering Karunagappally (CEK) is a premier public institution of engineering and technology, located in the serene town of Karunagappally, in the north-western region of Kollam district, Kerala, India. Established in 1999 by the Government of Kerala, CEK is the second engineering college in Kollam district and the fourth under the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD).

Accreditations & Recognition
  • Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU)
  • Approved by AICTE and accredited by NBA
  • Part of World Bank-aided TEQIP since 2012
  • IEEE MGA Regional Exemplary Student Branch Award — 5 consecutive years

Organizer

IEEE SB CEK

IEEE Student Branch College of Engineering Karunagappally (STB03951) is one of the pioneering student branches in the IEEE Kerala Section, officially established as a recognized organizational unit on 13 May 2009.

Since its inception, IEEE SB CEK has been a powerhouse of student-led innovation, technical excellence, and impactful community engagement — a recipient of several prestigious global awards that testify to its outstanding contributions to the IEEE mission.
