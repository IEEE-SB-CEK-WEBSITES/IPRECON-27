The Green Heaven

Welcome to Kerala

Nestled between the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats, Kerala stands as one of India's most picturesque states — known for its pristine air, clear blue skies, and lush landscapes that create a symphony of natural beauty.

Traveling across Kerala offers a glimpse into a way of life deeply influenced by its natural beauty, fostering a culture that is both serene and unhurried. Often referred to as "God's Own Country," Kerala captivates visitors with its breathtaking scenery, tranquil backwaters, and rich cultural heritage.

Kerala's backwaters form an intricate network of rivers, lakes, canals, and lagoons. Houseboat cruises — especially around Alappuzha, the "Venice of the East" — let travelers unwind amidst breathtaking landscapes.

Living History

Rich Cultural Heritage

A hub of global trade since antiquity, Kerala welcomed merchants from Phoenicia, Egypt, China, the Arab world, and Europe. This diverse influx shaped a unique cultural landscape where Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, and Judaism coexist harmoniously.

Historic Synagogue

Colour & Rhythm

Traditional Arts & Festivals

Kathakali — a highly stylized classical dance-drama blending mime, dance, music, and theatre — is recognized worldwide. Northern Kerala is famed for Theyyam, an ancient ritual performance of dance, music, and elaborate costumes.
