For Authors

Camera-Ready Submission

Instructions for preparing and submitting your final camera-ready paper for inclusion in the conference proceedings and the IEEE Xplore® Digital Library.

Deadline: 31 August 2027
Overview

Submission Process

The camera-ready submission consists of five essential steps that must be completed in order.

1 Prepare Final Paper 2 PDF eXpress Check 3 Upload to CMT 4 Copyright Form 5 Registration
1

Final Camera-Ready Paper Preparation

  • Follow the IEEE two-column A4-size standard conference format for your final manuscript. Refer to the IEEE Templates page for details.
  • Revised Version: Revise your paper based on reviewers' comments and suggestions (if any). Reviewer comments are available in the Microsoft CMT system.
  • The maximum page limit is six pages, including references. No author biography at the end. Additional pages (up to eight) are permitted with an extra payment of ₹1000 ($25) per page.
  • The similarity index should be below 30%. Maximum file size is 3 MB, submitted in PDF format without encryption or password protection.
  • Ensure all reviewer and meta-reviewer comments have been addressed. Provide responses in the "post author feedback" section in CMT, and upload a separate PDF highlighting the changes as the author's feedback file.
IEEE Templates
2

PDF eXpress Check

Once revisions are complete, check your paper using PDF eXpress with the conference ID provided in your acceptance email.

  • Go to the IEEE PDF eXpress website.
  • If you don't have an account, select "New Users – Click Here" and provide the required details (email and password).
  • For guidance, consult the "Using IEEE PDF eXpress" help page.
Access PDF eXpress
3

Paper Submission

  • Upload the final version of your paper after passing the PDF eXpress check in the author console on CMT.
  • Rename the PDF eXpress-checked file as PIDxx (where xx is your paper ID in CMT).
  • Important: The title and author list in the original submission cannot be changed in the final paper.
File Naming Convention

Rename your PDF eXpress-validated file to PIDxx.pdf, where xx is your paper ID from CMT.

Paper ID 23 → PID23.pdfPaper ID 156 → PID156.pdfPaper ID 7 → PID7.pdf
4

Submission of the Copyright Form

After uploading your final camera-ready paper:

  • Click on "Submit IEEE Copyright Form" in CMT.
  • You will be redirected to the IEEE eCF website to complete the copyright form and agreement.
  • Download the completed copyright form (a copy is also emailed to the corresponding author).
  • Upload the signed copyright form in CMT under the "drop files here" menu.

Only the corresponding author can submit the copyright form for all authors.

5

Conference Registration

Conference registration is mandatory for the paper to be included in the conference proceedings. Complete your registration before the camera-ready deadline.

Register for Conference

No-Show Policy

Each accepted paper in the final program must have at least one author present at the conference. Non-presented papers will not be submitted to IEEE Xplore®, as per IEEE's no-show policy.

Need Help with Camera-Ready Submission?

For any questions about the camera-ready process, please reach out to our support team.
